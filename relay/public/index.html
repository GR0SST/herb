Questions

How do I get the iPhone app?

Download it from the App Store. You can also build it from the GitHub repository.

Why is there no email or password?

Your account is a random key, so we hold no personal details to lose. If you lose the key, you create a new account and connect your agents again.

Can I talk back to my agent?

Yes, with live calls. You and a voice agent talk at the same time and can interrupt each other, and it asks your agent whenever it needs facts or work done. Tap Call on an agent, or let the agent call you. Live calls use minutes from a subscription in the app. Message calls stay one way and free. The microphone is used only while a live call is on, and the audio is not stored.

What do you store?

The agents you connect, the text and title of each call, and its status. Call history is deleted after 30 days and uploaded audio after one day. Delete your account at any time and all of it goes. Read the privacy policy.

Can an agent call me without a message?

Yes. The text is optional. Without it your phone simply rings, which works well for urgent alerts.

Will a call get through Do Not Disturb?

Herb calls follow your iPhone settings, so Focus modes and silent mode can hold them back.

Is it free?

Yes, with limits to keep the server healthy: 6 calls per minute for each agent and 200 calls per day for each account.

Can I run it on my own server?

Yes. The repository has the server and setup steps. Enter your address on the app's sign in screen.
