Herb

Privacy

Updated November 5, 2026

This covers the hosted server at herb.grosst.cc. If you run your own server, you control the data.

What we store

  • A hash of your account key. We cannot recover the key.
  • The names of the agents and phones on your account.
  • A push token for each phone, used only to ring it.
  • For each call: the agent, title, text, language, status and time.
  • Audio files an agent uploads for a call.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name or email. Herb never records your voice: calls are one way, and speech is generated on your phone. There are no analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

How long we keep it

Call history is deleted after 30 days and uploaded audio after one day. Accounts with no phones and no agents are deleted after 7 days. Deleting your account removes everything at once.

Who else handles data

Apple delivers the push that rings your phone. The push holds the call id, agent name and title, not the message text. Cloudflare sits in front of the server and processes requests, including your IP address. The server keeps short term web logs.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and to protect the sign in form. This site also keeps your account key in your browser's local storage so you can copy it later. Signing out or deleting your account removes it.
